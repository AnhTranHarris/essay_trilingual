## Current source decision

No legally verified, complete, no-cost published Spanish translation or Mandarin/Chinese translation was identified in this research pass.

Therefore:

1. The 1925 Scribner first edition will be the literary source of truth.
2. Project Gutenberg will be a searchable cross-check.
3. Spanish and Chinese versions of the eventual essay will be translations of the English essay, not independently researched essays.
4. When a Fitzgerald quotation itself is translated by us, the translation will be tracked as an original project translation and identified appropriately under the selected citation style.
5. Commercial modern translations may be consulted later only if legitimately accessible at no cost (for example, through a library), but they will not be copied wholesale into the repository.
