## Current source decision

The project now has a usable zero-cost three-language source architecture:

1. **English:** 1925 Scribner scan + Project Gutenberg transcription.
2. **Spanish:** 2014 Editorial Digital / Imprenta Nacional de Costa Rica edition, free official PDF.
3. **Mandarin Chinese:** 2013 Li Jihong Simplified-Chinese translation via no-cost Open Library borrowing, with the Zhou Yan translation as an independent QA comparison.

This enables the English essay, if analytically useful, to compare how selected metaphors move from Fitzgerald's English into Spanish and Chinese rather than treating translation as a mechanical afterthought.

The translated essays will still remain translations of the English analytical argument. Published Spanish and Chinese passages may be cited as source evidence where legitimately accessed and verified.
