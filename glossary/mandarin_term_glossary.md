## Freud terminology

Use conventional Mainland forms in prose:
- *Beyond the Pleasure Principle* -> 《超越快乐原则》
- *Civilization and Its Discontents* -> 《文明及其不满》
- repetition compulsion -> 强迫性重复

The Works Cited entries continue to identify the English editions actually cited.

## Citation rule

Parenthetical source identity and page numbers continue to refer to the English source editions used by the English essay.

Chinese quotation wording is a project translation unless explicitly stated otherwise.
