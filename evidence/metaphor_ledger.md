## Verification states

- DISCOVERED — candidate located but not checked against page image
- TEXT-VERIFIED — exact English wording checked against 1925 page image
- CITATION-VERIFIED — chapter/page citation checked
- ES-VERIFIED — corresponding Spanish passage checked
- ZH-VERIFIED — corresponding Chinese passage checked
- CROSS-LANGUAGE-VERIFIED — translation observations checked against all relevant source texts
- APPROVED — eligible for later essay use

## Translation-shift vocabulary

Use precise descriptions rather than vague judgments:

- literal preservation
- idiomatic substitution
- metaphor retained
- metaphor weakened
- metaphor intensified
- ambiguity narrowed
- ambiguity expanded
- imagery shifted
- register elevated/lowered
- connotation added/lost
- grammatical agency shifted
- cultural substitution
- no material semantic shift

## Hard rules

1. Nothing below APPROVED may be treated as final evidence in the essay.
2. Translation difference is not automatically translation error.
3. Do not reconstruct quotations from memory.
4. Do not paste complete copyrighted translations into this ledger.
