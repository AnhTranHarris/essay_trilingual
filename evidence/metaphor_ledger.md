## Classification note

The green light remains an important supporting symbol but is **not** counted as one of the seven strict metaphor anchors.

GG-M-003 deliberately distinguishes the larger “fantastic farm” metaphor from the embedded “like wheat” simile.

GG-M-006 deliberately distinguishes the spatial metaphor of recoverable time from the personification of the past as something “lurking.”

- DISCOVERED — candidate located but not checked against page image
- TEXT-VERIFIED — exact wording checked against 1925 page image
- CITATION-VERIFIED — chapter/page citation checked

## Hard rules

1. Nothing below APPROVED may be treated as final evidence in the essay.
2. Do not reconstruct quotations from memory.
3. The English novel is the sole literary authority.
4. Spanish and Mandarin translations of the eventual essay do not create new literary evidence.
5. Any translated Fitzgerald quotation must map back to an APPROVED English evidence ID.
